## Core brand language

Cerul has **one brand promise** and **one company positioning line**. They answer
different questions and must not be presented as two competing slogans.

### Brand promise — what Cerul enables

- EN: **Help AI understand, remember, and access video.**
- ZH: **让 AI 理解、记住并调用视频。**

Use as the primary headline where people first meet the brand: website hero,
login and signup brand panel, deck cover, launch screen, event key visual, and
brand advertising. Login and signup always share this same promise; only their
functional form copy changes.

### Company positioning — what Cerul is

- EN: **Video memory infrastructure for AI products.**
- ZH: **AI 产品的视频记忆基础设施。**

Use as a compact descriptor in About, footer, company and repository descriptions,
fundraising, recruiting, press, and sales materials. It may sit below the brand
promise as supporting copy, but must not compete with it typographically.

## Product campaign lines

## Product copy guidance

Product descriptions are normal explanatory copy, not slogans. Keep them factual
and adapt them to the surface while staying within these ideas:

- API / platform: understand video, retain context, and retrieve the right moment
  on demand through one API.
- Desktop: search what you have watched and heard, then return to the exact moment.
- Search / Evidence: return exact moments with source and timestamp evidence.
- Physical AI: turn experience captured on video into machine capability.

Recommended bilingual reference copy:

- API / platform EN: **One API to understand video, retain context, and retrieve the right moment on demand.**
- API / platform ZH: **通过一个 API 理解视频、保留上下文，并随时调用准确片段。**
- Desktop EN: **Search what you've watched and heard, and return to the exact moment.**
- Desktop ZH: **搜索你看过、听过的内容，直接回到准确片段。**

## Assembly rules

- **Max one slogan per screen.** The company positioning and normal explanatory
  copy may support it, but do not stack multiple campaign lines.
- On a company-level surface, use the brand promise. On a product-specific surface,
  use either the brand promise or the matching campaign line according to the page goal.
- When both core lines appear, the brand promise is the headline and the company
  positioning is the descriptor.
- The launch lockup may visually split the Cerul wordmark from the brand promise.
  Its accessible name is `Cerul. Help AI understand, remember, and access video.`
- **Never expose "index / 索引"** in user-facing copy. Express local-first as
  "on your own machine / 全在本机".
- Repository description formula: `Cerul — video memory infrastructure for AI products. <product noun phrase>`.
- English `access video` and Chinese `调用视频` are an intentional translation pair:
  English stays natural while Chinese conveys API-style availability.
